Mobile Operator for Mac

Whisper into your iPhone, the text is typed on your Mac. Everything runs on your Mac; nothing leaves it.

1. iPhone app

On this Mac? Point the iPhone's camera at the code.
On the iPhone? Open the TestFlight invitation.
TestFlight is Apple's app for test versions; it installs first if you do not have it.

2. Mac app

Download Mobile Operator for Mac


Apple silicon, macOS 14 or newer. A few MB; the speech model downloads during setup. The app updates itself afterwards.

3. Set up

  1. Open the downloaded Mobile-Operator.dmg and drag Mobile Operator onto Applications, as the picture shows. Then eject the disk.
  2. Open it. It is signed and notarized by Apple, so it opens with a normal double-click.
  3. A setup window opens and shows what is missing, each with a button. It closes itself when everything is green. A ◌ icon stays in the menu bar.
  4. Grant both permissions in System Settings → Privacy & Security: Input Monitoring (to see the key) and Accessibility (to type the text). Then quit and reopen the app.
  5. On the iPhone, open Mobile Operator and follow its four screens. Keep Wi-Fi and Bluetooth on, on both; no cable needed (a cable works too). The Mac setup window shows the iPhone as connected.
  6. Hold right ⌥ on its own, whisper into the phone (bottom edge about 5 cm from your mouth), release. The text is typed where the cursor is.

Settings…

Hold key and hold time, microphone (the iPhone, or any Mac input), model (small.en by default; large-v3 hears whispers best, 3.1 GB), vocabulary, and a History tab that keeps every dictation as audio + text with an Export button.

Log file: ~/Library/Logs/WhisperKey Lite.log.
